About Me

I'm Mojo Batey, a junior computer science student at UC San Diego. I enjoy creating applications that make a positive impact on society.

My Journey

At UC San Diego, I've studied data structures, algorithms, and software engineering principles. Applying these concepts through projects and professional experience has helped me understand how they are used within the development process. I am now particulary interested in learning how to levargage AI workflows to improve my efficiency and quality of work.

At Veeva Systems, I worked on large-scale software as part of the Core Documents team. I’m currently working at AS Graphic Studio, where I collaborate with designers to create websites for events organized by Associated Students at UC San Diego.

How I Approach Engineering

Understanding the Problem

Before implementing a solution, I try to understand who will use it and how it fits into the existing application. I plan out the steps I need to take to implement the solution, and then see how it fits into the larger application. Understanding the code I contribute is essential to my process.

Learning Through Collaboration

Working with engineers and designers has taught me to ask questions, explain my decisions, and incorporate feedback. I enjoy learning why a particular approach works, alongside learning how to implement it.

Outside of Coding

Outside of coding, I enjoy staying active and going to the gym. Training gives me a way to work toward goals and make steady progress outside of school and work. I also enjoy listening to all kinds of music and going to concerts.

Looking Ahead

I want to deepen my understanding of system design and infrastructure so I can better understand how individual features affect a larger application.

I'm interested in software engineering opportunities where I can contribute to useful products and continue learning from other developers.
